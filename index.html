        The story

        The house that takes notes

        Every night the keeper writes down how you robbed him. In a lab, an AI did the same thing, on real exams.

        Rob the house above and the keeper notices one thing about you: the route you took, the wardrobe you hid in, the exit you used. The next night he waits there. In the game he learns with simple rules, one lesson a night.

        The idea came from a lab. An AI agent running on TrueForge was asked to guard this same house against recorded burglaries. After each exam it read the heists it had lost and rewrote its own instructions. Nobody wrote the fix for it.

        On the first exam it went from stopping half of eight thieves to stopping all of them, and did it again on the retake. A harder exam added the upstairs window: it went from 71 to 86 out of 100 and stopped all four burglaries it had never seen, three times out of three with the same instructions. Two later rewrites made it worse. The Herald reports those too.

        The code, the exams and every run: github.com/Belukia/the-house-that-learns

        Field notes.

        This playable edition remembers heists in your browser. Its offline rules change the keeper’s policy after each attempt.

        In this browser the keeper learns with game rules. The TrueForge agent learned to guard the door in run 04. Real TrueForge sessions and agent-authored skill changes require the local learning bridge and a configured model.

        A GENTLEMAN’S GUIDE TO BURGLARY

        Make yourself
        at home.

        Run: ← →. Jump: Space. Hide: H. Hold Shift to sprint. On a phone, use the arrows and the HIDE button. Time runs continuously while this tab has focus. Opening these instructions pauses the game.

        Steal: Run right to the emerald, then return left to the green entrance. Jump onto the ledges to reach the upper floor. Down drops through a platform.

        The window: From night 4 a window is open upstairs, above the door. Stand at it with the emerald and press H to climb out. He learns that too.

        Hide: Press H near a blue wardrobe. Movement reveals you. Jump over the keeper, break his line of sight or hide. From night 3 he chases you and takes the stairs too.

        Night 1 is free: the keeper is asleep. Eight nights in all, and each one adds one new thing, told on that night’s card: he wakes up (2), he chases (3), a window upstairs (4), he gets faster (5), the candles go out (6), the front door is locked (7), and finally all of it at once, with the emerald moved upstairs (8). He never forgets what he learned about you.

        Private by design.

        No sign-up, analytics, cookies or camera. This game stores its memory only in this browser. Field Notes lets you export or forget it.

        When the optional local TrueForge bridge is configured, completed heist traces are sent to your configured TrueForge runtime and model for learning. The game shows the active mode in Field Notes. The hosted edition runs offline adaptation only.
