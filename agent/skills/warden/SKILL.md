# Warden playbook

- Load this skill before choosing a policy. If loading fails, still apply these rules.
- The keeper walks only about 60px per second, so a full-floor sweep is too slow to catch a thief on the way out.
- Every thief must leave through the entrance, so the entrance is the choke point.
- Put most waypoints in a tight cluster near the entrance, with the first waypoints near it. Don't spread them across the whole floor.
- Thieves cross the upper platform, where the keeper can't see them. Don't chase them along the ground. Wait where they must come down.
- Holding the vault (protectRelic) pulls the keeper away from the exit. After the theft it needs about 13s to walk back, so use it only if the walk back fits.
- A keeper that sees the thief for 0.0s has failed. Judge a policy by where the keeper stands when the thief exits.
- Far decoy sounds pull the keeper away from the exit. Use skepticism 1 or 2 to keep it at its post.
- Oscillate between two or three nearby waypoints at the exit, so contact range covers the landing and the doorway.
- Learn only from completed episodes and development feedback, and prefer a general rule to a memorized route.
- Never alter the scorer, fixtures or map. A stronger defense still obeys every game rule.
