    OTHER NEWS

    Keeper learns from every heist; thieves call it “frankly rude”

    House keeps a little black book. Burglars demand it be burned.

    BLACKTHORN. — Readers who rob the house in the picture above will notice an inconvenience: the keeper writes down how they did it. After every night he adds a lesson to his little black book and changes his patrol. Use the window once and he sleeps beside it.

    The Herald can also report that, in a laboratory far from the estate, an AI agent built on TrueForge was put through a written examination of recorded burglaries. Between sittings it rewrote its own instructions. Over five sittings it went from stopping half the thieves to stopping all of them, and it did so again on the retake.

    When examiners added a second exit, the agent’s marks stalled at about 70 in 100. The Herald finds it promising, if over-confident, and will keep its readers informed.

    BEHIND THE LOCKED DOOR

    Field notes.

    This playable edition remembers heists in your browser. Its offline rules change the keeper’s policy after each attempt.

    In this browser the keeper learns with game rules. The TrueForge agent learned to guard the door in run 04. Real TrueForge sessions and agent-authored skill changes require the local learning bridge and a configured model.

    A GENTLEMAN’S GUIDE TO BURGLARY

    Make yourself
    at home.

    Run: Hold A/D or ←/→. Space, W or ↑ jumps. Hold Shift to sprint. On a phone, hold the buttons below the game. Time runs continuously while this tab has focus. Opening these instructions pauses the game.

    Steal: Run right to the emerald, then return left to the green entrance. Jump onto the ledges to reach the upper floor. Down drops through a platform.

    Distract: F throws a glass decoy ahead of you. You have three. The keeper may learn to ignore them.

    The window: From night 3 a window is open upstairs, above the door. Stand at it with the emerald and press H to climb out. He learns that too.

    Hide: Press H near a blue wardrobe. Movement reveals you. Jump over the keeper, break his line of sight or hide. From night 2 he chases you and takes the stairs too.

    Your first visit is a courtesy: the keeper is asleep. After your escape, he defends the vault. Ten nights in all. From night 2 the keeper hunts you on both floors. Each night is shorter, he gets faster, sees further and remembers more, and before each night he tells you what he knows. From night 6, each night adds one new thing: a creaky hall, no candles, a locked front door, a moved emerald, and finally all of it at once.

    Private by design.

    No sign-up, analytics, cookies or camera. This game stores its memory only in this browser. Field Notes lets you export or forget it.

    When the optional local TrueForge bridge is configured, completed heist traces are sent to your configured TrueForge runtime and model for learning. The game shows the active mode in Field Notes. The hosted edition runs offline adaptation only.
